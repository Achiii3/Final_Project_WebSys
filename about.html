About StudySpot

A community-driven guide to the best places to study — quiet corners, cozy cafés, and safe public spaces near you.

Our Mission

We help students and lifelong learners find comfortable, safe, and productive places to study. StudySpot collects real user experiences so you can choose the right spot for focused work, group study, or casual reading.

How StudySpot Works

Suggest

Anyone can suggest a spot using the Suggest form — add a short description, tags, and an optional photo.

Review

Visitors can leave ratings and reviews on each spot’s details page. Reviews are stored locally and shown immediately.

Discover

Browse the Spots page to filter by tags like Quiet, Wi‑Fi, and Safe, or search for places near you.

FAQ

Are suggestions moderated?
Currently suggestions are stored locally and visible to you. If you want server-side moderation or public listing, we can add that later.
How are ratings calculated?
Ratings are averaged from submitted reviews and shown on each spot. On the Spots page we display the aggregated average and review count.
